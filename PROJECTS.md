# Projects

The projects shown on the portfolio, exactly as they are written in `src/data.js`.

## 1. DRISHTI

- **Category:** Biomedical · Embedded AI
- **What it is:** Wearable knee-response screening: motion and foot-pressure sensing feeds a Random Forest model that outputs a LOW / MODERATE / HIGH risk-marker category.
- **Core idea:** Multimodal assessment: movement, plantar loading, function, pain and recovery combined, not one sensor. Mobile app built; not clinically validated.
- **Stack:** ESP32, MPU6050, FSR, Random Forest, BLE, FlutterFlow
- **Live link:** not added yet

## 2. Unfold

- **Category:** Generative AI · Web app
- **What it is:** Turns reference documents and spoken explanation into presentations, with live voice-driven slide suggestions the presenter approves or rejects.
- **Core idea:** Connects document understanding, slide creation, speech recognition and presenter-approved live assistance in one app.
- **Stack:** LLMs, Speech-to-text, RAG, Next.js, FastAPI
- **Live link:** not added yet

## 3. CertifySecure

- **Category:** Document intelligence · Trust
- **What it is:** Certificate verification platform: OCR extraction, anomaly flagging and institution record matching for old paper and new digital certificates.
- **Core idea:** Combines legacy document intelligence with trusted digital verification in a single workflow.
- **Stack:** OCR, OpenCV, AI/ML, PostgreSQL, QR verification
- **Live link:** not added yet

## 4. Career Launch

- **Category:** AI · Career tech
- **What it is:** One living career profile that drives role matching, skill-gap roadmaps and tailored resumes. India-first.
- **Core idea:** Everything runs off a single approved profile instead of five disconnected tools.
- **Stack:** Next.js, FastAPI, LLMs, Resume parsing, PostgreSQL
- **Live link:** not added yet

## 5. No Tech Zone

- **Core idea:** Coordinated hardware and software workflow for communicating and managing phone-use policy, with emergency access kept open.
